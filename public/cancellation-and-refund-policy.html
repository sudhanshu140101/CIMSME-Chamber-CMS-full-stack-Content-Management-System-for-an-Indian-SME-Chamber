CANCELLATION AND REFUND POLICY

We Chamber of Indian Micro Small and Medium Enterprises take pride in the services delivered by us and ensure your satisfaction with our services and support. All fees paid at our website www.indiansmechamber.com are non-refundable under any circumstances. Once an order is placed, it cannot be canceled. However, in case you are not satisfied with our services, please contact us immediately at info@indiansmechamber.com and we will correct the situation.

The decision of the Chamber of Indian Micro Small and Medium Enterprises as regard to refund will be final.

We value your trust and are committed to providing exceptional service. If you have any questions or require further assistance regarding our cancellation and refund policy, please do not hesitate to reach out to us at info@indiansmechamber.com

All disputes are subject to Delhi Jurisdiction.
